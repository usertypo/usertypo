1. Who We Are

usertypo is a free online typing platform built for people who care about speed, accuracy, and the feel of every keystroke. Whether you are warming up for work, chasing a personal best, or racing a friend in real time, usertypo is designed to stay out of your way and keep the focus on typing.

We ship the Service at usertypo.com, with real-time multiplayer at mp.usertypo.com. The product is operated by usertypo_.

2. Our Story

usertypo started with a simple frustration: we loved typing tests, but most of them felt either too bare or too cluttered. Solo practice was easy to find. Smooth, modern multiplayer—with a UX that felt intentional instead of bolted on—was harder.

So we set out to build the site we wanted to use ourselves: a multiplayer-first typing experience with a clean, contemporary interface. From day one the goal was not just “another WPM counter,” but a place where you could race friends, climb leaderboards, track progress over time, and still enjoy a quiet solo session when you needed one.

Late nights turned into prototypes. Prototypes turned into rooms, dual matches, profiles, and themes. On July 27, 2026, usertypo officially went live—and we have been iterating ever since.

3. Mission & Values

Our mission is to make typing practice feel fast, fair, and genuinely fun—whether you are alone at the keyboard or racing someone across the world.

  • UX first. Clarity, responsiveness, and comfort matter as much as raw features.
  • Fair play. Leaderboards and races should reward skill, not cheats or noise.
  • Accessible by default. Core typing and racing features stay free so anyone can improve.
  • Open and honest. We ship in the open, document how the product works, and listen to feedback.

4. What We Build

usertypo combines classic typing practice with social and competitive layers:

  • Timed and word-count typing tests with languages, punctuation, and numbers
  • Real-time multiplayer rooms and dual matches
  • Profiles, XP, streaks, and detailed session stats
  • Friends, notifications, and online presence
  • Leaderboards with privacy-aware visibility controls
  • Themes, sound packs, and customization that stay out of the way until you want them

Features continue to evolve. If something feels missing, tell us—we read contactusertypo@gmail.com and in-app contact messages carefully.

5. Design Philosophy

Typing is a rhythm. The UI should protect that rhythm, not interrupt it. We aim for a modern look with deliberate motion, strong focus states, and layouts that keep the caret and text as the hero of the screen.

Multiplayer should feel instant and readable under pressure. Settings should be powerful without dumping every option onto the first screen. When we add something new, we ask whether it helps you type better—or just makes the page louder.

7. Open Source

usertypo is open source under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPLv3). You can read the code, fork it, and propose improvements.

  • Repository: github.com/usertypo/usertypo
  • Contributing guide: CONTRIBUTING.md
  • Security reports: SECURITY.md

Making the repository public lets anyone read and fork the code. It does not grant strangers deploy access, database access, or the ability to push directly to production. Changes land through review and Pull Requests.
